Hanging the inside piece was easier because I could literally use the same screw that held the previous one. Syncing the system was far easier than I thought it would be. (Just remember to have batteries on hand as they are not provided.) It took an hour or so before the outside monitor synced the humidity but the temperature was reading almost instantly after connecting. I have only one regret. Because this is battery operated and not plugged into the wall (like my previous station) it doesn't have an illuminated background. I also bought a very inexpensive lighted clock that now sits on my bathroom counter that is illuminated so, problem solved. All in all I am very happy with this little gadget and did a little snoopy dance because I was actually able to get this tiny bit of technology assembled, and up and running!" 1.
