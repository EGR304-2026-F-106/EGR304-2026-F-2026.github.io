## Introduction

A block diagram is an organized way to relate different parts of a system in a way that clearly delineates the purpose and structure between and within the component systems, and it can be worked on concurrently. Featured below is the block diagram for the HeatSmith device, demonstrating the general form of the two main components: the heat/humidity/light sensor(s), and the LCD screen. The block diagram's sections separate components based on their relationship to the microcontroller, with the left section featuring the components that feed data into the microcontroller and the right section featuring components that receive data from the microcontroller. Omitted are marginal/debug features such as a debug LED, all of which are featured in the HeatSmith team members' individual schematics. The diagram is otherwise feature-complete, and completely fulfills our product requirements by both gathering, processing, and displaying environmental data.
